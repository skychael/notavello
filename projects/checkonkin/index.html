CheckOnKin — Scheduled safety check-ins

CheckOnKin helps families stay connected through scheduled check-ins and caregiver alerts. It is designed to provide reassurance without making everyday communication complicated.

Simple check-ins

Family members can use clear, scheduled check-ins that fit into an ordinary day.

Missed check-in alerts

Caregivers can be notified when an expected check-in is missed, so they know when to follow up.

Privacy-conscious

The service is built around awareness without invasive location tracking, cameras, call logs, or screenshots.

Clear consent and support

CheckOnKin provides straightforward SMS consent information and accessible support resources for families and caregivers.

Visit CheckOnKin

CheckOnKin is an awareness tool for families, not a medical or emergency service.
